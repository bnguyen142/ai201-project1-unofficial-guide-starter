Supernote Planning Workflow

This article details how to use the Supernote as an integrated planning system that combines handwriting with digital calendar synchronization.

Key Planning Steps:

Foundation Setup:
Import a yearly planner template (e.g., My Deep Guide's yearly planner) into Supernote, then duplicate it for yearly, monthly, weekly, and daily spreads.

Handwriting to Digital Bridge:
Rather than maintaining separate systems, users can "turn handwritten notes into events" that sync directly to Apple Calendar through a custom workaround. This eliminates the need to switch between analog and digital planning tools.

Routine Structure:
- Monthly planning: duplicate templates and set goals
- Weekly spreads: handle scheduling and habit tracking
- Daily pages: capture tasks and reflections

Review Process:
Conduct periodic reviews at week or month's end, marking completed items and archiving pages.

Why This System Works:
This hybrid approach provides "the pen-and-paper feel of handwriting" combined with "the structure of a yearly template" and "the power of digital sync with Apple Calendar."

The methodology eliminates the need to redraw layouts repeatedly while ensuring nothing gets lost between analog note-taking and digital scheduling — allowing users to enjoy tactile handwriting satisfaction without sacrificing organizational reliability.

Bullet Journal Application:
- Import BuJo-style templates as PDFs into Supernote
- Use daily log pages for rapid logging of tasks, events, and notes
- Monthly and weekly spreads work well as Supernote notebooks
- Use headers and keywords to navigate quickly between spreads
